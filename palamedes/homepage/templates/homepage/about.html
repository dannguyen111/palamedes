About Palamedes

Built by Greek Life, for Greek Life.

Our Story

Palamedes started as a passion project by Dan Nguyen (former President) and Rajwat Singh (former Treasurer) of the Sigma Chi chapter at Gettysburg College.

Realizing that our Computer Science skills could be used somewhere helpful for our fraternity, and noticing the distinct lack of a shared management system for our chapter operations, we decided to take matters into our own hands. Dan built the initial structure of the website, and Rajwat joined the team shortly after to help bring the full vision to life.

Our ultimate goal is to share this service with other Greek organizations so they can overcome the exact same administrative hurdles we faced.

What is Palamedes?

Palamedes is a comprehensive chapter management platform designed to replace scattered spreadsheets, endless group chats, and disjointed payment apps with a single, unified hub.

By centralizing your organization's data, Palamedes empowers executive boards to lead effectively while keeping active members engaged and informed. Core features include:

Financial Management

Easily track member dues, send invoices, and manage chapter ledgers from a centralized dashboard.

Points & Participation

Submit, approve, and seamlessly track house points, event attendance, or community service hours.

Member Directory

Maintain an up-to-date, secure roster of all active members and their contact information.

Executive Tools

Equip chapter officers with role-specific access to oversee operations without administrative bottlenecks.
