About

Built by Greek life,
for Greek life.

Palamedes replaces the spreadsheets, group chats, and payment apps a chapter accumulates with one place to run the whole operation.

Our story

Palamedes started as a passion project by Dan Nguyen (former President) and Rajwat Singh (former Treasurer) of the Sigma Chi chapter at Gettysburg College.

Realising our Computer Science skills could be put to work for the fraternity, and noticing the distinct lack of a shared management system for chapter operations, we decided to take matters into our own hands. Dan built the initial structure of the site, and Rajwat joined shortly after to help bring the full vision to life.

Our goal is to share this with other Greek organisations so they can clear the same administrative hurdles we faced.

What you get

Financial management

Track member dues, take card payments, and keep the chapter ledger in one dashboard instead of four apps.

Points and participation

Submit, approve, and track house points, event attendance, and community service hours — with an audit trail behind every entry.

Member directory

A current, private roster of every member with the contact details officers actually need.

Officer tools

Role-specific access so each officer can run their own portfolio without waiting on anyone else.
